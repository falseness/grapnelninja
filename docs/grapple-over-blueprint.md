# Grapple Over replacement blueprint

This is reservation data for eight sections and 32 encounter briefs, not a playable mountain or proof of fun. `grapple-over-blueprint.json` is the authoring source. It deliberately does not load into index.html. The localhost replacement preview remains available; the legacy mountain and its 1080/1620 range remain the default. No activation, input, camera, gravity, pull, bounce, save or attached-rope changes are made.

The structural identity moves from industrial loading bays through a quarry, freight machinery, broken infrastructure and cable galleries to an exposed summit. Each landmark is a noncolliding silhouette. Only surfaces with an explicit traversal, recovery or challenge purpose may become colliders. The design removes filler pillars and repeated narrow intermediate pads: broad shelves separate decisions, blue faces are timed anchors, and yellow stays below the nominal ascent. Green shelves still bounce; they are planning approaches, not a promise of stationary safe standing. Later implementation must check stable observation poses or provide purposeful solid observation aprons for movers and red.

Each section has introduction, practice, variation and combination. Introduction in S06 and S08 means a new arrangement of known mechanics, not a new mechanic. S01/S02 use green only; blue begins S03; yellow S04; 540 and corner routing S05; red S07. The S05 upgrade occurs before its first encounter. S08 adds no mechanic. The S05/S07 optional E02-to-E04 connections each skip only E03; both leave the first-use encounter mandatory. Their reach and recovery are planned, not earned routes.

Coordinates are new reservations, not scaled legacy coordinates. Each encounter owns a 6000 by 3200 planning cell. Four cells form each 12800-high section; eight sections occupy 102400 vertical units. These deliberately generous bounds reserve space, not mandatory travel distance or a difficulty claim. Builders should compact unused space after local validation, never infer that 3200 is a calibrated jump. The west 2600-wide fall lane, 1600-wide ascent lane and east 1400-wide recovery lane have 200-unit separators. Ascent is monotone in y; S05 and S08 have disjoint bands, so neither folds back into the other. Section transitions and east return hooks require later local traversal proof. Every recovery graph edge is labelled planned and descends to the preceding encounter or base; acyclicity establishes connectivity only.

Numeric envelope ledger:

- TASK-560 supplies 60 panel thickness, 160 by 80 side-anchor faces, minimum 360 landing length, opening 540 recovery width, and the three exact patterns: opening gap/dx/dy/release 252/180/180/30 at 360; base 216/120/180/24 at 360; upgraded 324/180/180/30 at 540. Reach is hypot(dx+80,dy-40), below the corresponding radius. Broad planning shelves use 540 by 60. Release ticks describe an isolated fixture, not timing guaranteed on sloped or moving geometry. Jump variants require fresh placement checks.
- TASK-561 supplies blue 160 by 80 faces, 720 travel and 10000 ms period. A future placement must reserve the complete sweep and validate its phase. No claim that these dimensions alone guarantee a combined transfer.
- TASK-562 supplies yellow 2400 by 600, 600 clearance and 3800-wide catch. The blueprint places the band in the fall lane and the catch 600 below its bottom, with the nominal ascent east of it. That calibration used range 540. S04 has 360: fall braking is a reference, but its return hook spacing must be separately calibrated. This is explicitly unproven reservation data, not an assertion that 540 recovery works at 360.
- TASK-563 supplies red 80 by 80, 700 vertical travel, 6000 ms period, 500 observation length and measured minimum swept clearance 150. Future translated/combined layouts must retain at least 150 clearance and check the full swept rectangle against all obstacles. The blueprint does not pretend an unplaced sweep has passed that test.
- Camera remains 0.375. Preview base/upgrade acquisition, shot travel, held-shot reach and range circle remain 360/540. Attached rope retains existing behavior with no new snap limit.

The map is a labelled reservation schematic, not a production screenshot. Its rectangles show corridors and planning shelves; the PNG renders the same map for inspection. Automated checks establish data completeness, numeric correspondence, graph structure, image decode and representative isolated production movement with trusted input. They do not establish fun, continuous gameplay, all 32 playable encounters or end-to-end recovery.
